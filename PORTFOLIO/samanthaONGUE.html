A Bit About Me

Designing with care, coding with clean fundamentals.

Here's a quick look at who I am, what I enjoy building, and how I approach web design.

I am a 25-year-old developer living in Cebu City, Philippines. I got into front-end development because I love taking an idea or design concept and turning it into an interactive web page that people can actually click, test, and use on any screen.

I keep my focus on what matters most: writing semantic markup, building flexible layouts with CSS Grid and Flexbox, and adding straightforward JavaScript without unnecessary bloat. I pay close attention to the small details—making buttons feel responsive, keeping typography easy on the eyes, and making sure mobile views are seamless.

I'm an Aries with a genuine drive to build things well. When I'm away from my code editor, I enjoy discovering new design trends, spending time around Cebu, and learning new front-end techniques to sharpen my skills.
